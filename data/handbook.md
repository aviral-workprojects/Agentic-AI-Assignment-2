# Student Handbook

## Fee Payment
Tuition fees are paid per trimester through the online payment portal. Fees are due by the date shown in the student's fee record. Payments made after the due date attract a late fee of Rs 500 per week. Part payments are allowed only with written approval from the Accounts Office.

## Payment Portal Failure
If the payment portal fails or is down on the due date, the student may pay on the next working day without any late fee. The student must raise a support ticket within 24 hours with the transaction reference or a screenshot of the failure. Without a ticket, the late fee applies.

## Scholarships
Merit scholarships are awarded to students with a CGPA of 8.0 or above at the end of the previous trimester. Need-based scholarships require an income certificate and are reviewed once per academic year. Scholarship amounts are adjusted against tuition fees and are not paid in cash. Scholarships are renewed every year subject to maintaining the required CGPA.

## Course Change
Students may change an elective within the first two weeks of the trimester, subject to seat availability and programme chair approval. After two weeks, course changes are not permitted except on medical grounds with documentation. Core courses cannot be changed.

## Attendance
A minimum of 75 percent attendance is required in each course to be eligible for end-term examinations. Students below 75 percent must apply to the programme office for condonation, which is granted only for medical or approved institutional reasons.

## Support Requests
Support requests are raised through the support ticket system. Tickets are acknowledged within one working day and resolved within five working days. Include a clear subject and all relevant references such as transaction IDs.
